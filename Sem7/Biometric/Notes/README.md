## A note on coverage

Sections marked **[textbook fill]** — iris scan, retina scan, ear scan, palm print, AFIS — are
syllabus topics with **no dedicated slide deck** in `Resources/`. They are written from the
prescribed texts (Nanavati et al.; Jain, Flynn & Ross). Everything else is traceable to the
decks. `Behavioral Biometrics.pdf` also assigns **video face** and **mapping the body technology**
as Assignment 2 rather than lecturing them; working definitions for both are in
`04-Unit3-Behavioral-Biometrics.md` §6.
